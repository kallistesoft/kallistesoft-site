Math app · grades 1 to 6

Mathnut

10 to 15 minutes of math a day, at the right level, following what your child sees at school. Right answers earn acorns, and acorns buy the rewards you choose.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

How it works

A short daily session

10 to 15 minutes a day. A first check-up sets the level, then a check-up every week keeps it right.

The school program

Grades 1 to 6 in Alberta, British Columbia, Ontario, Québec and the United States. You tick what was seen at school.

Acorns and rewards

Every right answer earns acorns. Your child spends them on rewards you set up yourself.

Made for families

Several child profiles on the same device, and read-aloud for children who don't read yet. Children never see prices or purchase screens: everything for parents is behind a parent code.

No ads, no account

Names and progress stay on your device. Backups and transfers between a parent's phone and the family's tablets go by file or QR code, never over the Internet. The optional Mathnut Plus subscription (monthly or yearly, 14-day free trial) is handled by Google Play and the App Store.
