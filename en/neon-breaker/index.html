Neon Breaker

The arcade brick breaker, gone neon. Aim, smash, chain combos, and face a boss every fifth level. Every level has its own shape, and it gets harder each time.

App Store · coming soon Google Play · coming soon Play in your browser

What's waiting for you

Every level is different

Pyramids, invaders, fortresses, waves, mazes: 16 shapes, 7 color themes, plus steel, explosive and moving bricks.

A boss every 5 levels

The Sentinel, the Hive, the Prism, the Titan. Each attacks its own way, and they come back stronger.

Power-ups to catch

Wide paddle, multi-ball, lasers, slow motion, extra life. Red capsules make your life harder.

It gets serious fast

Faster ball, shorter paddle, tougher bricks: each level asks a little more. Game over? You restart from the level after the last boss you beat.

Free, no interruptions

Neon Breaker is ad-supported, but an ad never shows while you play: only between levels. No account needed.
