BetterHunter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy covers BetterHunter, the hunting exam and rules app for Android published by Kallistesoft. In short: there is no account, we collect no personal information ourselves and there are no analytics. Ads are served by Google AdMob and the app's settings come from Firebase. This page explains what each of them processes.

1. Data kept on your device

Your progress, answers, statistics and preferences (sound, language) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. They are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

BetterHunter shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a single full-screen ad after a finished test, never during a question. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information, and app-interaction and diagnostic data. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), Google's consent platform (User Messaging Platform) asks for your consent. You can change your choices at any time in Settings › Privacy options. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings.

4. App settings (Firebase Remote Config)

At launch and whenever the app returns to the foreground, it fetches its settings (features on or off, minimum required version) through Firebase Remote Config. This request carries a Firebase installation ID (random, unique to each install, separate from the advertising ID and not linked to your identity) and your IP address. This data is used only to run the app.

5. Reporting a problem

The "Report a problem" button opens your own email app with a message about the question you were viewing. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use your email address and message only to reply, and delete the exchange once it is no longer needed.

6. Children

BetterHunter is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including Québec under the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, elsewhere in Canada, and the European Union), you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, and withdraw your consent. Since we keep no data about you beyond our email exchanges, most such requests concern Google for advertising and Firebase; we'll gladly help you make them.

8. Contact and person in charge of the protection of personal information

For any question, request or complaint about your personal information: contact@kallistesoft.ca. You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Changes

We will update this page if BetterHunter's practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Policies for Kallistesoft's other apps and website: kallistesoft.ca/en/privacy.
