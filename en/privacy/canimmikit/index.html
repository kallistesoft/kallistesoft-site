Privacy policy — CanImmiKit

Last updated: November 21, 2026

CanImmiKit is an independent app published by KallisteSoft (Quebec, Canada). It is not affiliated with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), the Government of Canada or the Government of Quebec.

In short

  • We have no user accounts and no server that receives your immigration applications. What you enter in "Mon dossier" (application type, dates, steps) stays on your device.
  • Your exam results, goals and settings stay on your device too.
  • The app uses a few Google services (statistics, crash reports, ads, notifications) and the app stores for purchases, described below.

What stays on your device

Mock exam and practice results, mistakes to review, question-of-the-day streak, chosen goals, language, applications tracked in "Mon dossier" and their reminders, cached copies of processing times, draws and news. None of it is sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Third-party services

Firebase Analytics (Google) — anonymous usage statistics: screens opened, exams started and finished, purchases, app version, device type, approximate country. Used to improve the app. A random installation identifier is used; no name, email or "Mon dossier" content is sent.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — when the app crashes: the technical error trace, device model, system and app version, installation identifier. Used only to fix bugs.

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — if you turn on Express Entry draw alerts, your device gets a notification token and subscribes to "topics" (for example, one category's draws, in your language). Alerts are sent to everyone subscribed to a topic: we don't know who you are. Turn alerts off in Settings to unsubscribe.

Google AdMob — the free version shows ads. AdMob may use the device's advertising ID, IP address and technical information to show, cap and measure ads. In Canada and elsewhere, a Google consent form lets you choose personalised or non-personalised ads; you can change your choice in Settings › "Privacy options (ads)" when the option is offered. On iPhone, the app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) before any personalised ad. The "No ads" purchase or Premium removes ads.

Purchases (Google Play, App Store) — purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google or Apple. We never receive your name or payment details; the app only receives confirmation of what was bought.

Official sources — the app downloads IRCC's public files directly (processing times, draws, news on canada.ca) and opens official pages in your browser. Like any website, those servers see the device's IP address; the app sends them no personal data.

These providers' policies: Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy), Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Notifications

"Mon dossier" reminders are scheduled on the device, with no server. Draw alerts go through Firebase Cloud Messaging (above). You can turn each off in the app or in the phone's settings.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing an immigration step. It is not directed to children under 13.

Retention and your rights

Data on your device stays there until you uninstall the app. Firebase data is kept under Google's default settings (at most 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics). You can write to us with any question, to ask for access to or deletion of your information, or to withdraw your consent: we will handle your request under Quebec's Law 25 and PIPEDA.

Contact

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: KallisteSoft — contact@kallistesoft.ca
