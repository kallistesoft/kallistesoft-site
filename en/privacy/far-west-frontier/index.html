Far West Frontier — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy covers Far West Frontier: Hunt & Build, the hunting and camp-building game for Android published by Kallistesoft. In short: there is no account and no login, and we collect no personal information ourselves. Your progress stays on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob, optional purchases go through Google Play, and the app uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played and Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes. This page explains what each of them processes. The game does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

1. Data kept on your device

Your game progress (hunter level, camp, buildings, inventory, orders, timers), your settings, your total play time and the daily ad and booster counters used by the game's rules, and whether you bought Premium or a pack are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. They are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Far West Frontier shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for a bonus (speed up a timer, refill energy, keep your bag after a knock-out, double a full yard's harvest), and a full-screen ad shown only after you deposit everything at your storage (never during play, none in your first ten minutes of play, then at most one every five minutes and eight a day). Ads for alcohol, gambling, dating and sexual content are blocked.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and interactions with ads and the app, including diagnostic data.

This processing is Google's responsibility. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), Google's consent platform (User Messaging Platform) asks for your consent at launch. You can change your choices at any time with "Privacy options" in the game menu. On Android, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings (Google › Ads).

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The optional purchases (the one-time Premium upgrade and resource packs) are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details; the app only learns which product was bought, and stores that on your device. After a reinstall, the app asks Google Play to restore Premium. Premium removes the full-screen ads; the optional rewarded videos stay available.

5. Gameplay analytics (Firebase Analytics)

The app sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see how the game is played and balance it: for example sessions and their length, level-ups, zones opened, buildings and crafts finished, orders and sales, animals and bosses hunted, ads watched and their placement, and purchases. These events are linked to a Firebase app instance ID (random, not linked to your name or email). Firebase also collects device information (model, operating system version, language, country derived from your IP address) and may use your advertising ID. We use these statistics only in aggregate, to improve the game. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If Far West Frontier crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report: the error and where it happened in the code, the app version, device model and operating system version, a Firebase installation ID (random, not linked to your identity) and your IP address. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs. They contain nothing that identifies you.

7. Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config)

At launch, the game fetches a small configuration from Firebase Remote Config, for example the minimum app version required to keep playing. This request uses a Firebase installation ID (random, not linked to your identity) and your IP address. The configuration contains no personal information and is not linked to your identity. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

8. Notifications

If you turn them on (Android asks for your permission), the game schedules local notifications on your device to tell you when a build, a craft or a night's sleep has finished, or a yard is full. They are created by the app on your device; no notification server and no personal data are involved. You can turn them off in the game menu or in the device settings.

9. Children

Far West Frontier is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases are protected by Google Play's purchase authentication. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (including Québec under the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, elsewhere in Canada, and the European Union), you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, and withdraw your consent. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes everything stored on your device. For advertising and analytics data, use "Privacy options" in the game, reset or delete your advertising ID (Google › Ads) and see Google's privacy controls. Since we keep no data about you beyond analytics statistics, crash reports and our email exchanges, most requests concern Google; write to us and we'll gladly help, including deleting the analytics data tied to your app instance.

11. Contact and person in charge of the protection of personal information

For any question, request or complaint about your personal information: contact@kallistesoft.ca. If you write to us, we use your email address only to reply, and delete the exchange once it is no longer needed. You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

12. Changes

We will update this page if Far West Frontier's practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Policies for Kallistesoft's other apps and website: kallistesoft.ca/en/privacy.
