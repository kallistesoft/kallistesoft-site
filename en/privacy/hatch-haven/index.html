Hatch Haven — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy covers Hatch Haven (on the App Store: Hatch Haven: Merge & Collect), the cozy merge-and-hatch game for Android and iOS published by Kallistesoft. In short: there is no account and no login, and we collect no personal information ourselves. Your progress stays on your device and in your own cloud save. Optional rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, optional purchases go through Google Play or the App Store, Firebase Analytics (only with your consent) helps us understand how the game is played, and Firebase Crashlytics reports crashes. The game does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

1. Data kept on your device and in your cloud save

Your game progress (board, pets, island, quests, timers), your settings and the daily ad counters used by the game's rules are stored on your device. To protect your progress, a copy is kept in your own cloud save: Google Play Games Saved Games on Android, iCloud on iOS. We never receive this data. It is deleted if you uninstall the app and delete its cloud save.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Hatch Haven shows only optional rewarded videos, served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), that you choose to watch for a bonus (energy, double coins, a faster nest). There are no banners and no forced full-screen ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (on iOS only if you allow tracking in the system prompt);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and interactions with ads and the app, including diagnostic data.

This processing is Google's responsibility. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), Google's consent platform (User Messaging Platform) asks for your consent at launch; you can change your choices at any time with "Privacy options" in the game's Menu. On iOS, the system asks whether the app may track you; you can change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings (Google › Ads).

4. In-app purchases (Google Play, App Store)

Optional purchases (energy packs, egg packs, the starter and supporter packs) are processed entirely by Google Play or Apple's App Store under their privacy policies. We never receive your payment details; the app only learns which product was bought, and stores that on your device. Egg packs contain random pets: their odds are shown on each pack before you buy.

5. Gameplay analytics (Firebase Analytics, with consent)

Only if you consent, the app sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can balance the game: for example sessions, merges, hatches, quests, ads watched and purchases (product only). These events are linked to a Firebase app instance ID (random, not linked to your name or email), with device information (model, system version, language, country derived from your IP address). Analytics never uses your advertising ID. Without consent, no analytics event is sent. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If Hatch Haven crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report: the error and where it happened in the code, the app version, device model and system version, a Firebase installation ID (random, not linked to your identity) and your IP address. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs.

7. Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules one local notification on your device to tell you when your energy is full. It is created by the app on your device; no notification server and no personal data are involved. You can turn it off in the game's Menu or in the device settings.

8. Children

Hatch Haven is intended for players 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases are protected by Google Play's and the App Store's purchase authentication. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (including Québec under the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, elsewhere in Canada, and the European Union), you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, and withdraw your consent. Uninstalling the app and deleting its cloud save removes your game data. For advertising and analytics data, use "Privacy options" in the game, your device's tracking or advertising ID settings, and Google's privacy controls. Write to us and we'll gladly help, including deleting the analytics data tied to your app instance.

10. Contact and person in charge of the protection of personal information

For any question, request or complaint about your personal information: contact@kallistesoft.ca. If you write to us, we use your email address only to reply, and delete the exchange once it is no longer needed. You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

11. Changes

We will update this page if Hatch Haven's practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Policies for Kallistesoft's other apps and website: kallistesoft.ca/en/privacy.
