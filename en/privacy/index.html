Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you use Kallistesoft's apps (including Block Daily, Neon Breaker, Void Raid, Neon Pop, Neon Snake, Neon Stack and BetterHunter) and the kallistesoft.ca website. In short: we don't ask you for an account and we don't collect personal information ourselves. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which processes some data described below.

1. Data kept on your device

Your scores, statistics, progress and preferences (sound, language) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. They are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our free apps show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Android advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • technical information: device model, operating system, language, interactions with ads.

This processing is Google's responsibility. See how Google uses data for advertising and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices: on iOS, the app asks your permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings (Google › Ads). Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), a consent form is shown and you can change your choice at any time from within the app.

3. Sharing results

When you tap "Share", the app prepares a short text (for example your daily score) and opens your device's share menu. Nothing is sent without your action, and you choose who receives it.

4. BetterHunter (Android)

Here is exactly what the BetterHunter Android app does.

Advertising. BetterHunter shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a single full-screen ad after a finished test, never during a question. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information, and app-interaction and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent. Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), Google's consent platform (User Messaging Platform) asks for your consent. You can change your choices at any time in Settings › Privacy options. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings.

App settings (Firebase Remote Config). At launch and whenever the app returns to the foreground, it fetches its settings (features on or off, minimum required version) through Firebase Remote Config. This request carries a Firebase installation ID (random, unique to each install, separate from the advertising ID and not linked to your identity) and your IP address. This data is used only to run the app.

Everything else stays on your device. Your progress and answers never leave your device. There is no account and no analytics. Questions: contact@kallistesoft.ca.

5. Block Daily (Android)

Advertising. Block Daily shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a full-screen ad between games (never during play, at most one every 90 seconds), an optional video you can choose to watch to continue a game, and a banner under the pieces. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information, and app-interaction and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Ads for alcohol, gambling, dating and sexual content are blocked.

Consent. Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), Google's consent platform asks for your consent at launch. You can change your choices at any time with "Privacy options" on the home screen.

Remove Ads purchase. The optional one-time "No ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; the app only learns whether the purchase was made, and stores that on your device.

App settings (Firebase Remote Config). At launch, Block Daily checks whether an update is required or recommended through Firebase Remote Config. This request carries a Firebase installation ID (random, unique to each install, separate from the advertising ID and not linked to your identity) and your IP address. This data is used only to run the app.

Everything else stays on your device. Your scores, streak and games in progress never leave your device. There is no account and no analytics.

6. Website

kallistesoft.ca uses no cookies and no analytics. It and the web versions of our apps are hosted by GoDaddy, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when they load.

7. Children

Our apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We delete these exchanges once they are no longer needed.

In BetterHunter, the "Report a problem" button opens your own email app with a message about the question you were viewing. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including Québec under the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, elsewhere in Canada, and the European Union), you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, and withdraw your consent. Since we keep no data about you beyond our email exchanges, most such requests concern Google for advertising; we'll gladly help you make them.

10. Person in charge of the protection of personal information

For any question or request about your personal information, or to file a complaint: contact@kallistesoft.ca. You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

11. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, for example when we add a new app or service. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
