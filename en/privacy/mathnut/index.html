Mathnut — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers Mathnut, the math practice app for children in grades 1 to 6 published by Kallistesoft (9536-3529 Québec inc.) on Google Play and the App Store. It is written for parents. In short: there is no account and no sign-in, there are no ads, and your children's names, answers and progress stay on your device. The app sends only anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase, and the optional Mathnut Plus subscription is handled by Google Play or the App Store.

1. Data kept on your device

Everything your family enters or earns in Mathnut is stored only on the device, in the app's local storage: the children's profiles and names, their grade and school program, what you ticked as seen at school, check-up results, progress, acorns, the rewards you set up and the ones redeemed, the parent code and the app's settings. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete a profile, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

2. Backups and transfers between devices

You can back up the app's data, or copy it between a parent's phone and the family's tablets, with a file or a QR code. The transfer goes directly from one device to the other: nothing is sent over the Internet, and we never receive a copy. A backup file contains your children's profiles and progress, so keep it somewhere you trust. If you choose to save it in a cloud service or send it by message, that service's own privacy policy applies to the copy.

3. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If Mathnut crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report: the error and where it happened in the code (stack trace), the app version, the device model and the operating system version. Like any Firebase service, the request also carries a random installation identifier created by Firebase and the device's IP address, which Google needs to deliver it. We do not attach any user ID, and a report never contains a child's name, answers or progress. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs.

4. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see which parts of the app work well and which need fixing, Mathnut sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase, for example: a session finished (the skill, the level and a score range), a check-up finished, a reward redeemed, a subscription started. Analytics also records a few events automatically, such as the first launch, along with the device type, operating system and app version, language, and the country or region estimated from the IP address.

These events never contain a name, an answer or anything a child typed. Collection of the advertising ID is turned off and ad personalization is disabled, so this data is not used for advertising. The events are linked only to random identifiers created by Firebase for the installation, not to you or your child. We look at them only as totals.

5. Update check (Firebase Remote Config)

At launch, Mathnut checks through Firebase Remote Config whether a newer version is required, so that an old version can ask you to update. The request carries the random Firebase installation identifier and the IP address; it is used only for this check.

6. What we don't collect

  • No names: profile names stay on the device.
  • No answers or exercises: what a child answers stays on the device.
  • No account, no email address, no phone number, no sign-in.
  • No advertising ID, and no ads at all.
  • No location: no GPS or precise location (only the country or region that Analytics estimates, see section 4).
  • No data sold, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

7. Mathnut Plus subscription

Mathnut Plus is an optional subscription that a parent can buy in the parent area, monthly or yearly, with a 14-day free trial. It is sold, billed and managed by Google Play or Apple's App Store under their own terms and privacy policies (Google, Apple). We never receive your payment details: the app only learns from the store whether the subscription is active. Discount codes are also redeemed through the stores. You cancel the subscription in your Google Play or Apple account. The terms of the subscription are in the terms of use.

8. Reminders

If you allow notifications, Mathnut can show a monthly reminder on a parent's phone. The reminder is scheduled on the device itself; nothing is sent to us or to a server for it.

9. Children's privacy

Mathnut is made for children, and we built it so that it collects as little as possible:

  • Children never need an account, and we do not collect personal information from them: their names, answers and progress stay on the device.
  • There are no ads, no advertising ID, no chat, and no way for a child to share anything with strangers.
  • Children never see prices or purchase screens. Everything meant for parents (settings, rewards, subscription, backups) is behind a parent code.
  • The only data that leaves the device is the anonymous crash reports, usage statistics and update check described above, processed by Google Firebase as a service provider.

We designed these practices with children's privacy rules in mind, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Google Play's Families policy, Apple's App Store guidelines, and Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25), whose most protective settings are the default in Mathnut. We do not claim any certification or seal. If you believe your child's information has reached us, contact us (section 12) and we will delete it.

10. How long data is kept

  • Data on the device: until you delete the profile, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Usage statistics: user-level Analytics data is deleted automatically by Google at the end of the retention period set in our Firebase project (at most 14 months); only anonymous totals remain after that.
  • Our email exchanges: deleted once they are no longer needed.

See Privacy and Security in Firebase for how Google processes this data.

11. Your rights and deletion

Depending on where you live (including Québec, elsewhere in Canada and the United States), you can ask to access, correct or delete your information or your child's, and withdraw your consent. Most data never leaves your device, so you can delete it yourself: delete a profile in the parent area, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling also resets the random Firebase identifiers, so new statistics can no longer be linked to the old ones. For anything else, write to us: we reply within 30 days.

12. Contact and person in charge of the protection of personal information

For any question, request (including deletion) or complaint about your or your child's personal information: contact@kallistesoft.ca, 9536-3529 Québec inc. (Kallistesoft), 529 4th Avenue, Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu, Quebec J2X 1X3, Canada. If you write to us, we use your email address only to reply. 13. Changes

We will update this page if Mathnut's practices change, and the date at the top shows the latest revision. If a change ever meant collecting more information about children, we would ask for a parent's consent in the app first.
