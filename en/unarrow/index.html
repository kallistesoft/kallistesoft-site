Free game · Android

Unarrow

Each arrow can only leave in the direction it points. Find the right order to clear the grid, one arrow at a time. And every day, a daily puzzle that is the same for everyone.

Get it on Google Play App Store · soon

Two ways to play

Levels

Grids that get denser as you go, from a first warm-up to puzzles that make you think before every tap.

Daily puzzle

A new grid every day, the same for every player. Come back each day to keep your streak going.

Share

Your result fits on one line, ready to send to friends or post.

Stuck?

A hint shows the next arrow that can leave. And if you make a mistake, an extra life lets you pick up where you left off.

Free, no account

Unarrow is funded by ads: a banner under the grid, and now and then an ad between two puzzles, never during one. A one-time purchase removes the ads. No account is needed.
