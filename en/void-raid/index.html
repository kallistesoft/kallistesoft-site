Void Raid

The arcade space shooter, gone neon. Drag to steer, your ship fires on its own. Every level sends a new enemy formation, and a boss waits every fifth level.

App Store · coming soon Google Play · coming soon Play in your browser

What's waiting for you

Every wave is different

6 formations, 5 movement patterns, enemies that shoot, dive or soak up hits, and meteor showers.

A boss every 5 levels

The Warden, Hive Mother, Vortex, Phantom, The Core. Each attacks its own way and enrages at half health.

Power-ups to grab

Stronger guns (up to five cannons), shield, extra life, and the bomb that clears the screen.

It gets serious fast

Faster enemies, denser fire, more dives: each level asks a little more. Game over? You restart from the level after the last boss you beat.

Free, no interruptions

Void Raid is ad-supported, but an ad never shows while you play: only between levels. No account needed.
